FAQ

What is Jasuja Hosiery?

Jasuja Hosiery is a manufacturer and trader of hosiery goods, thermal wear and cotton apparel, established in 1976.

Do you manufacture or only trade?

Both. We manufacture our own brands and also trade in well-known brands.

What products do you offer?

All types of hosiery goods, thermal wear and cotton apparel.

Which brands do you manufacture?

JH Thermals, Real Quilt and OEM production, along with many more.

Which brands do you trade in?

White Rose, Array, Sweet Touch, PaceOne, Tropical Rainwear, Pinklush, Kidley, Melini and many more.

Yes, OEM is part of our manufacturing. Call or message us with your requirements.

What sizes do JH Thermals come in?

JH Thermals are available in sizes 36 to 90.

Are JH Thermals for the whole family?

Yes. The range covers men, women and kids.

How should I wash thermals?

Machine wash at 30°C, as shown on the care label.

Can I bleach thermals?

No. Do not bleach thermals, as the care label indicates.

Can I iron thermals?

No. Thermals should not be ironed, per the care label.

What are thermals?

Thermals are close-fitting base layers worn under regular clothes to keep you warm in cold weather.

Can I wear thermals under normal clothes?

Yes. That is what they are designed for, as a snug layer under shirts, sweaters or trousers.

How do I choose the right size?

Measure your chest or waist, compare with the size range, or ask us and we will help you choose.

How should I store thermals?

Wash and dry them fully, fold them and keep them in a dry place.
