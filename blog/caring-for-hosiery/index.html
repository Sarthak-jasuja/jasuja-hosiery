How to Care for Hosiery and Keep It Lasting

2026-10-05

Check the care label first, since it tells you the right washing temperature.

Wash similar colours together, use mild detergent and avoid harsh scrubbing.

Dry in shade rather than strong sun, and store folded in a clean, dry place. Small habits like these add months to a garment's life.
