Winter Layering: A Simple Guide

2026-10-05

Layering keeps you warm better than one heavy garment. Start with a thermal base layer next to the skin.

Add a middle layer such as a sweater or fleece for insulation. Finish with a jacket or shawl to block the wind.

The advantage is flexibility. You can remove a layer when it warms up during the day.
