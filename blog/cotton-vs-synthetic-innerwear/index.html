Cotton vs Synthetic Innerwear

2026-10-05

Cotton is soft, breathable and comfortable for everyday wear, which is why it is popular for innerwear and basics.

Synthetic blends can dry faster and stretch more. The best choice depends on climate, activity and how sensitive your skin is.

For daily comfort in most conditions, many people prefer cotton apparel.
