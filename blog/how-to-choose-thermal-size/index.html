How to Choose the Right Thermal Size

2026-10-05

A good thermal fits close to the body without feeling tight. Too loose and it will not trap warmth. Too tight and it will feel uncomfortable.

Measure your chest or waist and compare with the size chart. JH Thermals are made in sizes 36 to 90, so there is a fit for every family member.

If you are between sizes, think about how you plan to layer, and ask the seller for advice.
