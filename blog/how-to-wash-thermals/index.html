How to Wash Thermals the Right Way

2026-10-05

Thermals last longer when washed gently. Machine wash at 30°C, as the care label on JH Thermals recommends.

Skip bleach, and do not iron them. Heat and harsh chemicals can damage the fabric and its stretch.

Dry them in shade and fold them once fully dry. Washed this way, your thermals stay soft and snug all winter.
