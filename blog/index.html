How to Wash Thermals the Right Way

Thermals last longer when washed gently. Machine wash at 30°C, as the care label on JH Thermals recommends.

How to Choose the Right Thermal Size

A good thermal fits close to the body without feeling tight. Too loose and it will not trap warmth. Too tight and it will feel uncomfortable.

Winter Layering: A Simple Guide

Layering keeps you warm better than one heavy garment. Start with a thermal base layer next to the skin.

Cotton vs Synthetic Innerwear

Cotton is soft, breathable and comfortable for everyday wear, which is why it is popular for innerwear and basics.
